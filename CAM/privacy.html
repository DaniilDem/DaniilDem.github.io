Remote Cam — Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

Remote Cam is an iPhone camera app that you can control from a Garmin watch. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: nothing leaves your devices, and we collect nothing.

No data collection

Remote Cam has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. The app does not send anything to the developer.

Camera and microphone

The app uses the camera to take photos and videos when you press the shutter on the iPhone screen or on your watch. The microphone is used only in video mode, to record sound with your videos. Nothing is recorded in the background or sent anywhere.

Photos

Photos and videos are saved to the Photos library on your iPhone. The app asks only for permission to add to the library: it cannot see or read your existing photos.

Your Garmin watch

The app talks to your watch through the Garmin Connect app on your iPhone, using Garmin's Connect IQ software. It sends the watch the camera status and, if you turn the watch preview on, small low-resolution preview images (at most 64×64 pixels) so you can frame the shot. These go only to your own watch. Garmin's handling of this connection is covered by the Garmin Connect privacy policy.

Data stored on your iPhone

The app remembers which watches you chose (their names and Garmin identifiers), so you don't have to pick them again. This stays in the app's storage on your iPhone and is deleted when you delete the app.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
